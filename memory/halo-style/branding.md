# Halo branding

*(as of 2026-11-07)* Shared with haloLesson.

- All art in a chunky Duolingo-like 3D style: flat colors, solid darker ledges, a rounded white gloss highlight.
- The **yellow halo** is the family mark: a flat SVG ring, two-tone yellow (`#ffc800` family) with a darker
  ledge and a rounded white shine.
- No character mascot.
- Duolingo green was "very too much duolingo" for the user, hence violet accent.
